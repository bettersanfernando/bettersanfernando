# BetterSanFernando Page-to-Data Readiness Matrix

This is the authoritative planning matrix for the approved BetterSanFernando information architecture. It assesses whether verified, frontend-safe data is sufficient for each canonical page concept; it does not treat an existing route scaffold, a private file, or source feasibility alone as publication readiness.

`SITE-ARCHITECTURE.md` remains authoritative for canonical routes, redirects,
route ownership, and top-level information architecture. This matrix is
authoritative for data requirements, availability, publication readiness, and
status. Its 52 rows are canonical page concepts only.

## Status definitions

- `READY` — verified frontend-safe data is sufficient to implement responsibly.
- `PARTIAL` — verified useful data exists but intended page coverage is materially incomplete.
- `NEEDS_RESEARCH` — required verified information is missing.
- `NEEDS_EXPORT` — canonical/private verified data exists but requires a deliberately safe public export.
- `NEEDS_DECISION` — a product, privacy, or publication decision is required first.
- `DEFER` — the page is not appropriate for the current implementation phase.

## Scope rules

- The top-level navigation has exactly seven items: Home, Services, Projects,
  Government, Transparency, About, and Contact. Statistics belongs under
  Transparency and is not an eighth top-level item.
- Projects are a bounded infrastructure/public-works subset, not a complete register of city projects or procurement. The existing list and detail pages use 239 verified project records and 334 evidence records.
- `AWARDED` is not `CONTRACTED`. Award, contract, notice to proceed (`NTP`), and `COMPLETED` each require distinct evidence.
- Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC), winning bid, contract amount, and actual expenditure are different measures and must never be collapsed into a generic cost or spending value.
- The population baseline is the PSA 2024 POPCEN: 35 barangays and 377,534 residents. The geography export supports city and barangay polygons, not project point locations.
- Legislation coverage is uneven: 11 executive orders, 6 ordinances, and 0 resolutions are exported. These are bounded captures, not complete historical series.
- Finance is present in canonical/private source and analysis files but is not frontend-exported. Person-level finance fields require privacy controls.
- Barangay secretary and BHERT data are deliberately not exported. BHERT contains person-level material and is not automatically publication-safe.
- Most Services categories still require San Fernando-specific verification of eligibility, requirements, fees, processing times, locations, and contacts. The published exceptions are a bounded set of 113 reviewed External services across six categories: 8 Business Services, 7 Disaster Preparedness, 19 Assistance Programs, 14 Social Welfare, 6 PWD Services, and 59 Health Services. Of the canonical Citizen's Charter's 255 External services, 142 remain unpublished; its 74 Internal services are outside the resident-facing Services scope and remain unpublished. CHO records external-22 and external-60 remain held and are not published. This is not a complete City Government service inventory.
- Private canonical data existence does not equal public readiness. Frontend code must consume the typed `src/data/civic/` access layer and public-safe generated data, not the private repository.
