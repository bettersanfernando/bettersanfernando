# BetterSanFernando Project

## Overview

BetterSanFernando is an independent civic transparency portal for the City of
San Fernando, Pampanga. It is being developed under the BetterGov.ph BetterLGU
initiative and currently remains in the planning and data-feasibility-audit
stage.

BetterSanFernando is an independent project. It is not the official website of
the City Government of San Fernando and should not be presented as an official
government service or publication.

## LGU

- **Local government unit:** City of San Fernando, Pampanga

## Purpose and Goals

The project aims to make verified local government information easier for the
public to find, understand, and use. Its initial goals are to:

- assess which civic and government data can be published responsibly;
- organize verified information around public needs;
- preserve clear links between published facts and their sources;
- provide an accessible, mobile-friendly public resource; and
- establish a maintainable foundation for future contributors.

## Initial Possible Scope

The possible scope includes public information about city officials,
departments, services, barangays, finances, procurement, legislation, projects,
contacts, demographics, and geographic data.

This scope is provisional and subject to the data feasibility audit. Inclusion
in this list is not a commitment to publish a category. Product scope will be
defined only after available sources have been assessed for authority,
currency, accessibility, completeness, and maintainability.

## Core Principles

- **Accuracy over quantity:** Publish less rather than publish information that
  cannot be supported or kept current.
- **Source important facts:** Preserve enough source context for readers and
  maintainers to verify material claims.
- **Prefer primary government sources:** Use authoritative government records
  where they are available and suitable.
- **Distinguish official and independent content:** Clearly label source
  material and never imply that BetterSanFernando is an official city channel.
- **Accessibility first:** Make content understandable and usable for people
  with different access needs.
- **Mobile-friendly:** Prioritize reliable use on phones and constrained
  connections.
- **Politically neutral:** Present civic information without partisan advocacy
  or promotion.
- **Minimal personal-data collection:** Avoid collecting personal data unless a
  clearly justified public-interest need is established.
- **Maintainability:** Favor sources, formats, and workflows that contributors
  can review and keep current.
